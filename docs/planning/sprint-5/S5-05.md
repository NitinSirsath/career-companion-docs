# S5-05 / COM-41 — Correlate sync outcomes and worker health

Status: implemented; see [execution report](execution-report.md) for final validation and explicit external evidence gaps. COM mapping is inferred pending confirmation.

## Goal

Provide actionable existing-stack diagnostics without exposing Gmail content or adding telemetry infrastructure.

## Implementation

- Request/job/attempt lifecycle, retry metadata, queue wait, mode, duration and checkpoint commit outcome fields.
- Complete unique-candidate dispositions including filtered/not-found/unknown; confirmed insertion counted before queue acknowledgment.
- Queue accepted/suppressed/unknown offers separated from downstream processing success.
- Email worker reports attempt/retry exhaustion and persists safe allowlisted errors, clearing them only on actual completion.
- Operational read-only SQL and recovery interpretation in operations.md.

## Acceptance evidence and limits

- Partial-page, duplicate-reference, redaction and failure-category tests.
- Worker registration plus queue/lease/backlog/AI ledger diagnostic queries documented.
- No email body, snippet, raw SDK payload, token, cursor or address is emitted in lifecycle telemetry.

No reset of preserved data, new infrastructure, broader product scope or cross-repository boundary changes.
