# S5-04 — Correlate Gmail sync outcomes and worker health observability

Status: proposed local ticket.

## 1. Goal
Provide accurate correlated lifecycle evidence of Gmail sync outcomes, failure outcomes, counts, retries, and worker health without a new observability platform.

## 2. Problem / Gap
Current logs lack complete sync start/finish correlation and hide partial insertion outcomes.

## 3. Proposed Implementation
Introduce a small structured event builder in `gmailSync.ts` and `emailProcessingJob.ts`. Capture specific candidate disposition counters.

## 4. Acceptance Criteria
- Sync outcomes and attempts are correlated with unique IDs.
- Operators can easily see retry metadata and queue wait times.
